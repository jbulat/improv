# Stories

Each story here began as a child's idea, quoted as they said it. Only the names are changed. The
`story-create` skill asked one to three questions, cast the story onto the troupe, and built it.
The device then performed it: every voice, costume and sound effect below is the same one the
StickS3 plays.

Each video plays with sound. Each story is also plain data: the **performance** link opens
the `performance.json` the device plays.

New to the troupe? [Meet them first](README.md): Miss Moon introduces all six actors in the
README's opening clip.

## Wilfred the Friendly Dragon

> Once upon a time there was a friendly dragon called Wilfred and she was meant to be a really
> mean dragon guarding a princess in a tower but she didn't want to be a Mean dragon she wanted to
> be a nice dog.
>
> Wilfred talked to the princess called Mia about wanting her to transform Wilfred into a dog but
> she said you need to meet a dog first so Wilfred set of to find her pen pal bob who was a dog and
> bob gave Wilfred advice to embrace being herself so when the Mia asked if he still wanted to do
> the spell she said he'd rather stay a friendly dragon. <3
>
> — child, age 11

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/51f2729e-b1e7-46dc-886f-c200ebb04fe2

A dragon who is very bad at being mean wants to be a dog instead. *66 seconds.*

**Watch for:** his roar at 0:13, which comes out as fire; and his very best woof at 0:41, which
comes out as fire again.

**Cast:** Wilfred — Tuk, with horns · Princess Mia — Rosa, with a tiara · Bob — Pip ·

## Lulu and the Fringe That Grew Back

> Once upon a time there was a girl called Lulu who had a really long fringe and every time she
> tried to cut it grow back then one day she went to this magical witch person and she made her
> bald and then slowly her hair go back and then her fringe was gone but then she still needed to
> use her clips with her hair was still really really really really really really really long
>
> — child, age 11

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d737c8ca-0a01-48fd-aa5e-0339749c182b

A fringe that grows back every time Lulu cuts it, until a witch tries a bigger spell.
*58 seconds.*

**Watch for:** "Higgledy, piggledy... BALD!" at 0:29, and Lulu at 0:34 with her fringe gone. The
fringe is a costume layer, so the spell simply takes it off.

**Cast:** Lulu — Ada, with a fringe · The witch — Rosa, with a witch's hat and a wand ·

> friends so they went to the pet store and got a dog called bear. He was brown or fluffy. Marisol

> ever after
>
> — child, age 7
